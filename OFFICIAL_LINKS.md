## How the sites relate

- **Chris Nugent** creates the apps. GitHub and itch.io are not the creators.
- **GitHub** stores source code and the public preview APK releases/website backup.
- **Netlify** hosts each of the two distinct live browser applications and their current signed Android APKs.
- **itch.io** lists the free apps publicly under the `qsrecovery` profile.
- **quietstrengthrecovery.co.uk** is the official project hub and the URL to share. `/start/` provides guidance; old `/testers/` links lead to `/downloads/`.
- **iPhone** visitors should open the browser apps; Android `.apk` files cannot be installed on iPhone.

### Current limitations

Current Android previews: Strength v1.9 and Sisters v1.3. Signed packages have been checked, but independent physical Android device acceptance testing remains outstanding. Google Play releases are not approved; these download links are **not** Play testing opt-in links. Browser/app storage are separate; users should back up private writing themselves.
