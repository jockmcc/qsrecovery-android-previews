Quiet Strength includes 365 original daily recovery reflections with ACOA themes, official AA/ACA reading links and 20 direct videos. Quiet Sisters retains its 365 family readings and family-support resources. Both have clearer Home shortcuts, reader controls and a searchable tool index.

The creators' own videos and official reading websites require internet access. Original app readings are bundled/cached for offline use after loading. These tools do not provide medical care, counselling or emergency support. Physical-phone acceptance of the new versions is not claimed.

GitHub stores source and signed releases. Netlify hosts the two browser apps.
