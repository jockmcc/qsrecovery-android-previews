My approach

Listen first. Offer tools. Respect choice.

I took useful bits from different recovery programmes and learnt what worked in my own life. I still use those lessons every day.

Sometimes a practical tool helps. Sometimes you need a conversation, a pause or somebody who understands. The apps offer things to try, without making recovery a test you have to pass.

“I can share what helped me. You decide what is useful for you.”

The PAUSE approach

Make room for the person.

PAUSE describes how I approach support. BRAVE is a practical guide within Quiet Strength Recovery. Use either in the way that helps you.

P

Provide safety

Start with calm, choice and clear boundaries.

A

Ask how things really are

Make room for an honest answer.

U

Understand

Notice feelings and patterns without labelling someone.

S

Share

Offer experience and options the person can accept or leave.

E

Encourage

Find one realistic next step.

About Chris

I've had to find my own way too.

I'm Chris Nugent. I've lived through bullying and addiction and built a life in recovery. I know how hard it can be to tell someone what is really going on.

My recovery and experience supporting people have taught me to listen, keep clear boundaries and take small steps seriously. QSRecovery brings those lessons into my writing and two free apps people can use in their own way.

The experience I bring

  • Completed AA and ACOA twelve-step programmes
  • A background in mental health support and the Prison Service
  • Level 2 Certificate in Counselling Skills
  • Level 2 Certificate in Awareness of Mental Health Problems

The apps offer self-guided tools and education from lived experience. They do not provide counselling or treatment.

I want this project to feel human, honest and approachable. Feedback from the people using it will help it grow.
